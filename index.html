About GibWeather

Release v1.8

Built for Gibraltar

GibWeather is designed around local conditions: Levanter and Poniente flow, gusts, low cloud, humidity, visibility and rain.

Forecast & observation sources

Weather and marine forecasts are provided by Open-Meteo under CC BY 4.0. GibWeather uses the best-match forecast plus the Marine Weather API and keeps a last-known-good forecast on your device for offline fallback.

Current airport observations come from Gibraltar Airport METAR reports (LXGB) supplied by the NOAA/NWS Aviation Weather Center. GibWeather retrieves these server-side and publishes a small same-origin observation file for the app.

Recent rain-radar imagery is loaded on demand from RainViewer's public Weather Maps API and displayed over OpenStreetMap tiles. Radar imagery is not stored as part of the GibWeather offline forecast.

MeteoGib

MeteoGib is linked as an independent local forecast source. GibWeather does not scrape or republish MeteoGib forecasts and is not affiliated with MeteoGib.

Install on iPhone/iPad

Once hosted over HTTPS, open GibWeather in Safari, tap Share, then choose Add to Home Screen.

  1. Open the GibWeather web address in Safari.
  2. Tap More/Share.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Turn on Open as Web App.
  5. Tap Add. GibWeather will then open in its own app window.

Privacy

GibWeather v1.8 has no account, advertising SDK or analytics tracker. The last successful forecast and your app preferences are stored locally on this device.

The Levanter and Rock Cloud indicators are experimental guidance only. For official or safety-critical weather information, use the relevant official authority or a qualified local forecaster.
